# Driver Guide

The driver portal (`/driver`) is designed for a phone. Sign in at `/login` with the email and initial
password the office gave you.

## What you see

- Only **your own** assignments: date and time, pickup and drop-off, itinerary, passenger count, and the
  customer's name and phone number (tap to call).
- The assigned vehicle: name, registration, seats, luggage space and any notes from the office.
- You do not see other drivers' work, prices, other bookings, fleet documents or the office's review notes.
- If you are not eligible (verification incomplete, licence or PrDP expired, account disabled), the portal
  tells you and collection is blocked. Contact the office.

## Collecting a vehicle

1. Open the assignment and tap **Start collection inspection**.
2. Answer every checklist item: **OK** or **Problem**. For a problem, describe it, take a photo, and say
   whether it is minor or makes the vehicle unsafe. Brakes, tyres, lights, seatbelts, steering and warning
   lights are always treated as safety problems.
3. Take the four vehicle photos (front, rear, left, right).
4. Take a fresh **collection selfie** with the vehicle. It is an accountability record only; no face
   recognition is used.
5. Enter the odometer and fuel level, then submit.

What happens next:

- **Safety problem reported:** do not take the vehicle. The office is alerted and will arrange another.
- **Approval required** (the default): wait until the assignment shows *Released to you* before driving.
- If the office asks you to redo the inspection, the step shows *Please redo this inspection*.

## Returning a vehicle

Tap **Start return inspection** when you bring the vehicle back. Complete the checklist and photos and
enter the odometer, which cannot be lower than at collection. New damage keeps the vehicle out of use until
the office has it checked.
