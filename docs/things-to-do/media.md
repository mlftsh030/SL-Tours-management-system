# Image manifest

Checked 2026-10-04. Every published listing and every vehicle option shows at least one picture. All pictures are from Wikimedia Commons under the licence shown, or are SL Tours' own photos, and are served from `apps/web/public/assets` (960 px, plus a 480 px version, cropped to 3:2).
EXACT means the picture shows the listed place. ILLUSTRATIVE pictures show the type of activity or vehicle, are labelled on the site ("Illustrative image—actual location may differ" or "…actual vehicle may differ"), and carry a caption saying what they actually show.
No videos were added. Video support (official, privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds that load only on click) is built but unused; adding one would also need `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com` in the site's Content-Security-Policy `frame-src`.
The Norval Foundation's own image links were not used because reuse permission is unconfirmed; a Commons photo is used instead.
