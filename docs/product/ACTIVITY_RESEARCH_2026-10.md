# Activity Research, October 2026

Research for the Explore catalogue was carried out on 2026-10-03. Every published figure was read on the cited page that day.
Figures change; each activity records its verification date and each price rule its effective dates where the source gives them.
Re-verify before relying on any figure after its effective period or after six months.

## Method

- Official operator pages first, then public authorities (SANParks, SANBI, Iziko, City of Cape Town) and Cape Town Tourism.
- Pages were rendered in a headless browser so that prices loaded by JavaScript (calendars, shops, accordions) could be read.
- Third-party listings and search-result summaries were used only to find official pages, never as evidence.
- Activities whose existence, location or hours could not be confirmed from an accessible official page stay **drafts**.
- `price_status` records whether prices are verified, set at booking, not published, or free.

## Verification gaps to close

Migration V15 records these in the data: conflicting figures carry an uncertainty note (estimates until resolved), Ice Station
weekday sessions apply to Tuesdays only, and Century Karting, the Ice Station and the Labia have their participation limits
marked as not or only partly published.


- **SANParks 2026/27 conservation fees** (Cape Point, Boulders) were not published on 3 Oct 2026; current fees end 31 Oct 2026. Itineraries dated from 1 Nov 2026 show these fees as unverified until an admin adds the new rules.
- **Century Karting** publishes no prices or age/height limits online; the pricing link was broken.
- **Groot Constantia** tasting prices and **District Six Museum** admission are only visible inside booking flows or not at all.
- **Neighbourgoods Market** parking is stated as both R10 and R20 on official pages; entry fee not stated.
- **Castle of Good Hope** last ticket sale is stated as both 15:15 and 15:45; 15:15 used.
- **Iziko** winter weekend hours (08:30-16:00) do not define the winter period.
- **The Ice Station** sessions were sampled for one weekday and one weekend; confirm that every weekday matches.
- **Cape Town Tourism** and the **City of Cape Town** document store blocked automated access, so Lion's Head and Muizenberg Beach remain drafts.
- **Cinema chains** (Ster-Kinekor, Nu Metro) do not publish readable ticket prices; drafts until confirmed.

## Planning assumptions (not research)

Travel times between areas are SL Tours planning estimates: straight-line distance between area centres x 1.4 at 40 km/h plus 10 minutes, editable by administrators. They are shown with every itinerary and are not measured routes or live traffic.
