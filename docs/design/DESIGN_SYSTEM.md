# Design System

## Brand

SL Tours positioning: **Cape Town, through local eyes.**

The experience should feel premium, local, warm, and operationally trustworthy without becoming a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Typography

- Use system sans-serif for speed and legibility.
- Hero headings may be large on desktop but must remain readable on mobile.
- Compact panels and operations screens use smaller, dense headings.

## Color

- Ink: `#10222d`
- Ocean: `#083c5d`
- Ocean accent: `#0f5e7d`
- Fynbos green: `#2f6f4e`
- Coral CTA: `#d96c38`
- Sun focus: `#f4b653`
- Mist: `#eef6f6`
- Sand: `#f6f0e6`
- Success: `#18794e`
- Danger: `#b42318`

## Spacing

- Mobile page gutters: 16px.
- Main content max width: 1180px.
- Section rhythm: 48-72px vertical.
- Form/grid gap: 16px.

## Radius And Shadows

- Standard radius: 8px.
- Cards use restrained shadows only when they need separation.
- Avoid nested cards and decorative glass effects.

## Buttons

- Primary: coral background, white text.
- Secondary: mist background, ocean text.
- Ghost: transparent, white border for hero use.
- Minimum hit height: 44px.

## Inputs

- Labels are visible.
- Errors render close to fields.
- Inputs use 46px minimum height and clear focus states.

## Cards

Cards are for repeated tour items, metrics, portal tiles, and panels. Page sections remain full-width/unframed.

## Navigation

- Desktop: horizontal nav with `Book Now` and `My Trip`.
- Mobile: full-screen menu, large touch targets, and clear close button.

## Accessibility

- Use semantic landmarks.
- Maintain visible `:focus-visible`.
- Icons are decorative unless paired with accessible labels.
- Respect reduced motion.
- Avoid text over busy imagery without an overlay.

## Imagery

Use real SL Tours/Cape Town travel photography where possible. Avoid generic stock-looking hero treatments. Images should show the actual tour, place, or experience.

## Operations Patterns

Operations screens are denser and quieter than public pages: left navigation, metrics, responsive tables, status pills, and action-first information.
